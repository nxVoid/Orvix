# Image-generation provenance

The built-in image_gen tool was used through the installed imagegen skill. Selected outputs are copied into this workspace. Reference boards supplied art direction.

## Quantum Horizon landscape prompt

Production science-fiction profile banner. Cinematic matte painting with physically convincing fractured mountains, multiple layers fading through atmospheric perspective, obsidian basalt foreground, charcoal slate ridges, quiet cold grey-blue sky and distant violet-grey haze. A small controlled lavender energy fissure sits on the right horizon. The left lower third stays dark and quiet for avatar overlap. No stars, grid, floating rectangle, buildings, text, logos or UI. Compose for a 1440×480 crop.

Master is quantum_horizon/landscape-master.png. Runtime poster is resized and encoded as WebP through the existing Sharp dependency.

## Independent mist prompt

A single thin horizontal veil of naturally wispy grey-lavender ground fog on true transparent background, ultra-wide 3:1. A narrow lower-middle band fades on all edges. Sparse turbulence and photographic detail. No terrain, sky, stars, objects, lightning or text.

Master is quantum_horizon/mist-master.png. The separate alpha layer drifts six pixels over 24 seconds in the authored runtime SVG.

## Amethyst Crown prompt and refinement

Front-facing original avatar jewelry, truly transparent inside and outside. Fine brushed gunmetal circular mount, five naturally irregular faceted amethyst crystals above the outer rim, smaller supporting shards, independent plum/lavender facet highlights and slender metal settings. No neon, glow, particles, platform, background or text.

Refinement requested an exactly centered circular opening near 38 percent of square width in radius, an outer metal radius of 42 percent, and crystals confined to the top outer region.

Selected master is amethyst_crown/crown-material-master.png. Face protection exposed an art defect in upper mounts. The product remains an excluded candidate. Generated material and the initial vector source are retained for revision.
